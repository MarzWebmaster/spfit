# User Guide: Role Management

## Overview
The Role Management module allows administrators to control user access levels within the system.

## Standard Roles
The system comes with 4 pre-defined roles that cannot be deleted:

1.  **Admin**
    *   **Access**: Full control over the system.
    *   **Usage**: For system owners and high-level administrators.
2.  **Staff**
    *   **Access**: Internal employees.
    *   **Usage**: For support staff or operational teams.
3.  **Supervisor**
    *   **Access**: Management level.
    *   **Usage**: For team leads who need to oversee reports and other users.
4.  **Freelancer**
    *   **Access**: Restricted.
    *   **Usage**: For external contractors who only need to see their assigned tasks.

## Managing Roles

### Accessing the Panel
1.  Login as **Admin**.
2.  Navigate to **Tetapan** (Settings) in the sidebar.
3.  Select **Peranan & Kebenaran**.

### Creating a New Role
1.  Click the **"Tambah Peranan"** button.
2.  Enter the **Role Name** (must be unique).
3.  Enter a **Description**.
4.  Select the **Permissions** for this role.
5.  Click **Simpan**.

### Editing a Role
1.  Click the **Edit** button next to the role.
2.  Modify the name, description, or permissions.
3.  Click **Simpan**.
    *   *Note: System roles may have restricted editing capabilities.*

### Deleting a Role
1.  Click the **Padam** (Delete) button next to the role.
2.  Confirm the deletion.
    *   *Note: You cannot delete System roles or roles that are currently assigned to users.*
